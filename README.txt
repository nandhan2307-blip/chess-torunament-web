CHESS TOURNAMENT POINTS TABLE
=============================

Open index.html in any browser (keep index.html, style.css and app.js in the same folder).
Or just open chess-tournament-single-file.html, which has everything in one file.

Stages
  1. League: 8 players, 28 matches (Match 1 = SANJAY vs PRAVUU ... Match 28 = JUBAA vs SARAN),
     7 rounds of 4 matches, every player plays once per round.
  2. Semi-finals: 1st v 4th and 2nd v 3rd (top 4 of the league).
  3. Final (semi-final winners) and Third-place match (semi-final losers).

Enter a league result
  Type the match ("SK vs VIVEK" or "match 5"), pick the winner or Draw, click Save result.
  Or click "Enter result" in Fixtures, or any square on the Results board.

Enter playoff results
  Opens when all 28 league games are played. Click "Mark winner" on a player.
  If league results change later, playoff results that no longer match are cleared.
  Use "Adjust seeding" to settle ties.

Photos
  Click a player's picture in the Players section and choose a photo.
  Photos and results are saved in this browser on this computer/phone.
